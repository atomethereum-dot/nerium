How it works
  1. 1Lock on the origin networkYour wallet sends the amount to the bridge contract on the network you choose.
  2. 2Proof checked by NereumEVMThe deposit is verified before anything is released. If it does not check, nothing moves.
  3. 3Released 1:1 on NereumEVMThe same amount arrives at your address, ready for Spot, Swap or Pools.
